Efficiency vs. affinity

Your role is a Nen type. Affinity sets the ceiling.

In Hunter x Hunter, every user is born to one of six Nen types. You hit 100% in your own type, and lose about 20% for every step away from it. Tech works the same way. Pick your role below, then watch what happens when you try to work in someone else's corner, with or without AI.

The affinity hexagon

Tap a type to make it your born type. Every other corner dims to show how far your affinity carries you: 100 → 80 → 60 → 40. Opposite corners are the hardest crossings.

The AI law

AI is aura, not a type change. Handing someone an AI does not move them to another corner of the hexagon. It scales the affinity they already have.

A native Conjurer (frontend) with AI operates near 100%. An Emitter (infra) pointed at the same frontend work is still in the opposite corner, capped near 40%, no matter how much AI they pour in. The bucket is the same size; AI just fills it faster.

The lesson is not "learn everything." It is: find your corner, train it to 100%, and let AI multiply the affinity you were built for, instead of trying to buy an affinity you do not have.
